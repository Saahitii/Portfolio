05 / Perspective

What the data taught me.

Growth is useful only when it creates a sustainable audience and a repeatable process.

LEARNINGS

Reach is not the same as monetisation

My main YouTube channel previously earned ad revenue, but monetisation was removed under YouTube’s reused-content policy. Reach alone does not protect income. Original contribution — commentary, editing and context — needs to be part of the content strategy from the start.

Views are not the same as retention

My second Instagram account lost followers despite 52.2K views in 30 days, while my third YouTube channel had few regular viewers. I now look beyond reach and pay closer attention to returning viewers and follower conversion.
